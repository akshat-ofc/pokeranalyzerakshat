Help & definitions

Win % — percentage of simulated/enumerated outcomes where your hand wins outright.

Tie % — percentage where the pot is split between two or more hands.

Lose % — percentage where your hand loses.

Equity — your expected share of the pot, counting split pots proportionally. Equity is not the same thing as win probability.

Pot odds / required equity — the minimum equity you need for a call to break even, ignoring future betting rounds.

EV — expected value of calling under the stated assumptions (single street, no fold equity). See the assumptions listed with every EV figure.

Outs — remaining cards that upgrade your hand's category. Probabilities use exact combinatorics, not the "outs × 2/4" shortcut.

Every number in this app is a probabilistic estimate under the assumptions you selected (especially opponent ranges). None of it is a guarantee of any outcome. Please gamble responsibly, and only where legal in your jurisdiction.
